# Privacy and Safety

## Current Product State

Spanda is in a private pre-release stage.

Access is intentionally limited while product language, experience quality, and safety framing continue to improve.

## Safety Intent

Spanda is designed to support calm personal reflection with a respectful and non-judgmental experience style.

Core safety principles:

- User-first clarity
- Respectful, supportive language
- Minimal pressure and low-friction interaction
- Practical, manageable next steps

## Data Sensitivity Guidance (Pre-Release)

Until public policies are finalized, users should avoid entering highly sensitive data such as:

- Personal identifiers beyond what is necessary
- Financial account or payment details
- Government identification numbers
- Private credentials, passwords, or security secrets
- Confidential third-party information

## Responsible Use Boundaries

Spanda is a reflection companion, not a replacement for:

- Professional mental health care
- Medical advice or diagnosis
- Legal advice
- Emergency support services

If urgent or crisis-level support is needed, users should contact relevant professional or emergency resources immediately.

## Internal Sharing Guardrails

For internal demos and updates:

- Share product purpose and user value, not implementation details
- Remove or mask personally identifying information from screenshots
- Avoid publishing raw user reflections in open channels
- Restrict links and access to approved audiences
- Use approved wording that reflects private pre-release status

## Communication Safety Language

Recommended positioning language:

- "Private reflection companion"
- "Early-stage pre-release experience"
- "Focused on pattern awareness and gentle action"

Avoid public-facing claims that imply:

- Clinical treatment capability
- Guaranteed mental health outcomes
- Finalized public policy commitments before official release

## Pre-Launch Safety Checklist

Before public launch, finalize and publish:

1. Privacy policy and terms language
2. Acceptable use boundaries
3. Data handling and retention communication
4. User support and escalation pathways
5. Public-facing safety and crisis guidance

## Practical User Reminder

Spanda works best when users share reflective content thoughtfully and avoid posting sensitive or high-risk personal information during this pre-release phase.
