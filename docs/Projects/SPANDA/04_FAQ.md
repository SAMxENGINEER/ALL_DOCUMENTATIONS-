# FAQ

## What is Spanda?

Spanda is a private reflection companion that helps users understand recurring thought and action patterns through an evolving visual map.

## Where can I access it?

Current live link: http://spanda-one.vercel.app/

## Is Spanda fully public?

No. It is currently in a private pre-release stage.

## Who is Spanda best for?

People who want better self-awareness, more consistent reflection, and clearer follow-through between what they think and what they do.

## How much time should I spend daily?

Most users can start with 2-5 minutes per check-in, once or twice a day.

## Do entries need to be long?

No. Short and specific entries are usually more useful than long, unfocused writing.

## What should I expect after one week?

You should begin noticing repeated themes, recurring emotional loops, and places where intention and action align or diverge.

## Does Spanda replace therapy or medical support?

No. Spanda is a reflection tool and does not replace professional care, diagnosis, legal advice, or emergency services.

## What should users avoid sharing right now?

Users should avoid sharing highly sensitive personal data, financial details, private credentials, or confidential third-party information during pre-release use.

## How should I describe Spanda internally?

Describe it as a private, early-stage reflection experience focused on pattern awareness and gentle action.

## What makes Spanda different from a normal journal?

A normal journal stores entries linearly. Spanda emphasizes relationships and recurring patterns, helping users see how thoughts and actions evolve over time.

## What is the best way to start?

Start with one honest thought, one real action from today, and one small next step for tomorrow.
